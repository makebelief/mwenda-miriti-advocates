Insights

Questions worth asking early.

General information only. Legal advice should be based on the particular facts and documents of your matter.

Disputes

Preserve the record before the dispute escalates

Messages, contracts, receipts and other records may become important if a disagreement develops into a formal dispute.

Property

Due diligence before commitment

Property transactions should be checked before funds and signed documents move between parties.

Documentation

Changing a name is more than changing an ID

Deed-poll and registration steps create the formal paper trail needed to update records consistently.

Employment

Preserve the employment record

Contracts, correspondence, payslips, warnings and disciplinary documents may become important if a workplace dispute develops.

Succession

Start with the estate documents

Succession and probate questions often depend on family relationships, property records and the documents left by the deceased.

Commercial

Contracts should answer difficult questions early

Payment, performance, risk, termination and dispute clauses matter most when a relationship stops going smoothly.
